# Indulge logo exploration prompt

Mode: built-in image generation

> Use case: logo-brand. Design one original minimal symbol made from a single
> thick deep-navy rounded stroke forming an almost-complete circular attention
> loop. Let the bottom gently settle into a subtle cradle shape. Place one
> cherry-red solid dot precisely in the open gap at the upper-right, representing
> the conscious choice that completes the moment. Use a crisp flat vector style,
> iconic geometry with warmth, a full-bleed powder-sky blue background, generous
> negative space, and a silhouette that remains unmistakable at 29 px. Exactly
> one navy loop and one red dot. No person, furniture, clock, brain, leaf,
> checkmark, letter, text, wordmark, speech bubble, extra shapes, 3D, gradients,
> lighting, shadows, texture, border, rounded-corner mask, transparency, or
> watermark. Keep the composition safe for an Apple app-icon crop.

The generated exploration established the direction. The shipping geometry was
redrawn as `indulge-mark-v2.svg` so the curve, colors, opacity, and small-size
exports are deterministic.
