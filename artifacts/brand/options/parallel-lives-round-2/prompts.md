# Parallel lives logo exploration — round 2

Mode: built-in image generation with the first Parallel Lives concepts supplied
as visual references.

All six prompts requested a centered, flat vector-style iOS mark using a
Powder Sky field (`#E1F0FC`), Navy paths (`#0C1C38`), and exactly one Cherry
choice point (`#DB293B`). They excluded text, arrows, literal people, clocks,
brains, leaves, checkmarks, infinity/DNA shapes, gradients, shadows, textures,
borders, transparency, and baked-in icon masks.

## Variants

- **Crossover A — Compact Exchange:** two vertical paths exchange positions
  through one compact crossover, with the choice point at the exchange.
- **Crossover B — Quiet Switch:** two horizontal paths cross at a shallow angle
  and resume in parallel, like changing lanes rather than forming an X.
- **Bridge A — Side Step:** two parallel vertical paths are joined once by a
  short diagonal bridge, with the choice point halfway across.
- **Bridge B — Open Passage:** two parallel arcs share one small passage whose
  negative space reads as a doorway between lives.
- **Branch A — Gentle Fork:** one path divides once into two equally weighted
  routes, avoiding a literal tree, road sign, or letter Y.
- **Branch B — Revealed Route:** a second route emerges beside the habitual path
  from one choice point and ends parallel to it.
