The onboarding now feels cinematic, warm, and recognizably native. Its largest remaining opportunity is product breadth: most non-TV indulgences still receive an honest symbol treatment rather than a complete pose-and-prop scene.

## What Is Working

- The atmospheric MeshGradient connects the native chrome to the room without competing with the illustration.
- Typography has a clear emotional and functional split: rounded display moments, readable SF Pro Text elsewhere.
- iOS 26 glass is limited to compact navigation controls, with an equivalent iOS 18 material fallback.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Non-TV scene breadth remains abstract.** It preserves truth, but the emotional peak is strongest only for television. Add bespoke scene recipes one indulgence at a time rather than reusing the TV plate.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Private first-timer:** Identity questions are skippable and explained; no major red flag remains. The user may still wonder what happens after the final reflection.
- **Large-text user:** The full question and choices remain scrollable. Decorative wordmark and chapter chrome are capped so navigation stays intact.
- **Motion-sensitive user:** Travel becomes a fade, ambient breathing stops, and answer causality remains visible.
