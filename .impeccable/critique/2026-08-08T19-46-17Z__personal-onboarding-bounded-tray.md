## What improved

- The app opens directly on the name question instead of delaying interaction
  with a welcome preamble.
- The scene and current prompt share one viewport on phone.
- The primary action is pinned while dense answer lists scroll independently.
- The scene switches to the sofa/television composition before asking about
  television duration.
- Portrait iPad uses a wide stage above a constrained prompt instead of
  cropping the character inside a tall split pane.
