## Overall Impression

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The scene has no cinematic authorship

**Why it matters:** Detached capsule anatomy, weak hand-to-prop contact, uniform rounded blocks, and sparse contact shadows make the central promise feel cheap and juvenile.

**Fix:** Build one authored adult character and one hero living-room transformation with believable pose contacts, a tuned camera, layered lighting, tactile materials, and expressive timing before expanding the recipe library.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable bolder`, followed by `$impeccable animate`.

### [P0] The customer sees a review console, not onboarding

**Why it matters:** Replay, animation-mode, motion override, dropdown, scrubber, and five companion chips expose renderer controls instead of guiding recognition and desire.

**Fix:** Replace the screen with a full-screen sequence of emotional beats. Each screen has one thought, one action, and a scene that changes immediately as the consequence.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`.

### [P1] The emotional logic moralizes

**Why it matters:** “This is where your time goes” plus TV shrinking into a default books/yoga/art collage contradicts the promise to protect intentional pleasure.

**Fix:** Begin with recognition, visibly keep the chosen pleasure, and let the user select what reclaimed time becomes. Transform only the unwanted excess.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

### [P1] Controls outrank wonder and iPad is uncomposed

**Why it matters:** The yellow replay button and dark tray dominate the phone; iPad stretches a phone panel around a tiny stage in dead space.

**Fix:** Let the scene own most of every frame, reveal controls only when needed, and author a separate regular-width staging.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`.

### [P2] Accessibility is mechanical rather than emotionally equivalent

**Why it matters:** Reduce Motion removes travel but does not provide an equally satisfying storyboard; clipped chips and exposed sensitive choices are fragile at larger text sizes.

**Fix:** Design deliberate cuts, dissolves, pose changes, captions, and contextual sensitive declarations; test accessibility text sizes.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`.
