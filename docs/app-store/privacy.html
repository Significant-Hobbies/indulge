Privacy by default

Your inner life stays yours.

Indulge is a private, local-first iPhone app. Your profile, trades, reflections, Focus notes, and history remain on your device.

What the app stores

Indulge stores the choices you make during onboarding, your active trade, and the Focus entries you create in the app's local container. This information is not uploaded to us.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information, app activity, advertising identifiers, or private journal content from this version of Indulge. There is no account, advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. Apple may process beta diagnostics and feedback when you install through TestFlight under Apple’s terms.

On-device intelligence

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence may suggest bounded tags for a Focus note. Processing occurs on device. Manual classification remains available and your note is not sent to us.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Deleting Indulge removes its local app data. Because we do not hold a server-side copy or account, there is no remote profile for us to retrieve or delete.

Your choices

You may leave optional onboarding questions unanswered. Indulge does not currently request Screen Time authorization, and core use does not require network access.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds sync, accounts, or server processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released. For questions, visit Indulge Support.
